		<p>Support for parents is crucial for family well-being and quality of life. In parenting support sessions, we work together to understand the behaviours, emotions, and needs of children to improve family relationships and find solutions to everyday challenges.</p>
		<p>Some of the goals for these sessions are:</p>
		<ul class="list-disc pl-8">
			<li>Understanding the processes of child development, the stage your child is at, their level of play, skills, and difficulties.</li>
			<li>Discussing daily routines and your child's emotional needs.</li>
			<li>Talking about different parenting styles, their implications, and the balance between setting boundaries and being flexible.</li>
			<li>Obtaining guidelines on how to respond to certain difficult situations in daily life. How to negotiate or set boundaries in a way that aligns with what you desire. Working on children's emotional regulation starting from the emotional regulation of the adult.</li>
			<li>Discussing aspects of parenting related to high self-demands, ideals, feelings of being overwhelmed, etc.</li>
			<li>Setting clear and achievable goals according to what you want to achieve.</li>
		</ul>
		<p>These sessions aim to provide a safe space where parents can express their concerns and learn new strategies to face the challenges of parenting. The ultimate goal is to strengthen the parent-child relationship, improve the quality of family life, and promote a conscious and fulfilling parenting environment.</p>
